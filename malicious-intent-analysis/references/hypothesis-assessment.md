# Hypothesis assessment

Separate observation, impact and inferred intent. Hanlon's Razor prompts testing
ordinary errors; it neither establishes incompetence nor excuses harmful behavior.

For each substantive finding keep an evidence ledger:

| Observation / locator | Hypothesis supported | Counterevidence | Dependence | Missing discriminator |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Checkable behavior | Legitimate / accidental / harmful / unresolved | Strongest contrary fact | Independent or shared cause | Evidence that could change the inference |

Prefer "deliberate unauthorized collection in this artifact" to "malicious author."
Keep an unresolved explanation where named hypotheses are not exhaustive.

- Supported: specific evidence supports deliberate harmful/manipulative behavior
  and materially weakens alternatives.
- Suspected: support exists but important alternatives/discriminators remain.
- Inconclusive: evidence does not discriminate adequately.
- Not supported by reviewed evidence: bounded negative inference, not innocence.

High confidence in a traced harmful path can coexist with inconclusive intent.

## Bayesian discipline

Use structured updates for contested explanations, ambiguous accumulated signals
or assumption-sensitive conclusions. Numeric calculation is optional.

`posterior odds = prior odds * P(E | H1) / P(E | H0)`

For adequately specified mutually exclusive hypotheses:

`P(Hi | E) = P(E | Hi)P(Hi) / sum_j P(E | Hj)P(Hj)`

Do not pretend hypotheses are exhaustive. With dependent evidence, use a joint
cluster or likelihood conditional on evidence already considered.
No invented empirical base rates, equal priors by default, precise unsupported
posteriors, or double-counting a payload, decoded version and scanner alert.
Missing evidence is unknown, not automatically incriminating.

Prefer qualitative direction/strength updates when calibration is unavailable.
Explicitly label illustrative numbers as assumptions; test plausible ranges
and report conclusion reversals. Update only with new evidence. Stop at exhausted
evidence, not a desired answer. Stable uncertainty is convergence.
Publish concise reasoning and evidence, not private deliberation.

## Worked contrasts

Encoded image data consumed only by a renderer fits legitimate asset embedding.
Encoding adds little support to concealment. Report an Observation if material,
with bounded intent Not supported by reviewed evidence.

An install path reading sensitive environment values into an outbound client
demonstrates capability. "Diagnostics" is a competing explanation, not an excuse:
check authorization, minimization, destination and reachability. Report impact
independently; intent may remain Suspected or Inconclusive.

Illustration only: assuming a likelihood ratio of 4, a 1% prior gives about 3.9%
posterior while a 20% prior gives 50%. These are not measured values or a verdict.
They illustrate sensitivity to assumptions. Without defensible inputs, describe
qualitative support and uncertainty. Risk containment need not await certain intent.
